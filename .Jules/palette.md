## 2024-07-08 - Keyboard Interaction Consistency for Container Components
**Learning:** In Astro/Starlight (and web design generally), container components like `<Card>` and `<LinkCard>` often have hover effects (like transform and box-shadow) that are entirely missed by keyboard users navigating through internal links, creating an inequitable experience. Adding `:focus-within` directly maps these visual cues to keyboard navigation, and it's critical to ensure both `:hover` and `:focus-within` are included in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` queries.
**Action:** Always check container elements for `:hover` states and systematically duplicate or group them with `:focus-within` selectors to guarantee keyboard users receive equivalent structural feedback, ensuring both respect reduced-motion settings.
